## Article Types Overview

| Article Type | Description |
|--------------|-------------|
| **Original Research** | Full-length reports with complete methods |
| **Review** | Exhaustive literature review or provocative discussion |
| **Brief Report** | Concise report for extended research, additional data, confirmatory results, small-scale studies |
| **Comment** | Agenda-setting expert piece on topical issues |

## Brief Report Format Requirements

### Definition
> "A concise report of research including the presentation of research that extends previously published research, the reporting of additional data and confirmatory results, and small-scale studies."

### Word Limit
- **Không có word limit cụ thể** được nêu trong guidelines
- Brief Report thường ngắn gọn (ước tính: ~2,000-3,500 words)
- Khuyến nghị: Giữ dưới 3,500 words để phù hợp định dạng "concise"

### Abstract
- **150-250 words**
- Không chứa undefined abbreviations
- Không chứa unspecified references

### Keywords
- **4-6 keywords**

### References
- Citation style: **Numbered in square brackets** [1], [2-5]
- Reference list: Numbered consecutively
- Bắt buộc bao gồm DOIs đầy đủ nếu có
- Format mẫu: `Gamelin FX, Baquet G, Berthoin S et al (2009) Effect of high intensity intermittent training on heart rate variability in prepubescent children. Eur J Appl Physiol 105:731-738. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00421-008-0955-8`

## Manuscript Structure

### 1. Title Page
Cần bao gồm:
- **Title**: Concise and informative
- **Author names**
- **Affiliations**: Institution, department, city, state, country
- **Corresponding author**: Active email address
- **ORCID**: 16-digit ORCID của tác giả (bắt buộc cho corresponding author)
- **Running title**: <50 characters (khuyến nghị)
- **Acknowledgments**: People, grants, funds (funding agencies viết đầy đủ)

### 2. Abstract (150-250 words)
### 3. Keywords (4-6)
### 4. Main Text
- Font: 10-point Times Roman
- Italics for emphasis
- Không dùng field functions
- Sử dụng tab stops cho indents
- Headings: Tối đa 3 cấp

### 5. Statements & Declarations (SAU References)

#### Required Statements:

**a) Funding**
```
Example: "This work was supported by […] (Grant numbers […] and […])."
Example: "The authors declare that no funds, grants, or other support were received."
```

**b) Competing Interests**
```
Example: "The authors have no relevant financial or non-financial interests to disclose."
```

**c) Author Contributions**
```
Example: "All authors contributed to the study conception and design. Material preparation, 
data collection and analysis were performed by [full name], [full name]. The first draft 
of the manuscript was written by [full name] and all authors commented on previous versions. 
All authors read and approved the final manuscript."
```

**d) Data Availability** (BẮT BUỘC cho original research)
```
Example: "The datasets generated during and/or analysed during the current study are available 
in the [NAME] repository, [PERSISTENT LINK TO DATASETS]"

Example: "The datasets are not publicly available due to [REASONS] but are available from 
the corresponding author on reasonable request."
```

**e) Ethics Approval** (nếu nghiên cứu liên quan humans/animals)
```
Example: "This study was performed in line with the principles of the Declaration of Helsinki. 
Approval was granted by the Ethics Committee of [University] (Date.../No....)."
```

**f) Consent to Participate**
```
Example: "Informed consent was obtained from all individual participants included in the study."
```

**g) Consent to Publish**
```
Example: "The authors affirm that human research participants provided informed consent for 
publication of the images in Figure(s) 1a, 1b and 1c."
```

### 6. References
- Numbered consecutively
- Bao gồm DOIs đầy đủ

### 7. Tables
- Numbered using Arabic numerals
- Cited in consecutive numerical order
- Table caption explaining components
- Footnotes: superscript lower-case letters

### 8. Figure Captions
- Đặt SAU references, TRƯỚC tables
- Format: **Fig. 1** (bold) + caption
- Không có punctuation ở cuối caption

## Figures Requirements

### Technical Specifications

| Type | Format | Resolution |
|------|--------|------------|
| Vector graphics | EPS (preferred), PDF | - |
| Halftones (photos) | TIFF | 300 dpi minimum |
| Line art | EPS/PDF | 1200 dpi |
| Combination | EPS/PDF | 600 dpi |
| Color | RGB (8 bits/channel) | - |

### Figure Lettering
- Font: Helvetica hoặc Arial (sans serif)
- Size: 2-3 mm (8-12 pt)
- Contrast ratio: ít nhất 4.5:1

### Figure Size
- Single column: 84 mm (large journals) hoặc 119 mm (small journals)
- Double column: 174 mm
- Maximum height: 234 mm

### Figure Numbering
- Arabic numerals: Fig. 1, Fig. 2, etc.
- Parts: lowercase letters (a, b, c)
- Cited in consecutive numerical order

### Color Figures
- **Free of charge** for online publication
- Đảm bảo thông tin vẫn visible khi convert sang black & white

## Supplementary Information (SI)

- **Movies**: avi, wmv, mp4, mov, etc. (max 2GB)
- **Text/Presentations**: PDF format
- **Spreadsheets**: .csv hoặc .xlsx
- **Numbering**: ESM_1.pdf, ESM_2.mpg, etc.
- **Citations**: "...as shown in the animation (Online Resource 3)"

## Submission Checklist

### Before Submission
- [ ] ORCID của corresponding author
- [ ] Tất cả tác giả đã đồng ý nội dung và thứ tự
- [ ] Manuscript được proofread
- [ ] Không đang submitted ở journal khác
- [ ] Có cover letter cho Editor

### Files Cần Nộp
- [ ] Manuscript chính (.docx hoặc .doc)
- [ ] Figures (EPS/TIFF/PDF)
- [ ] Tables (editable, không phải image)
- [ ] Supplementary Information (nếu có)
- [ ] Cover Letter

### Format Kiểm Tra
- [ ] Abstract: 150-250 words
- [ ] Keywords: 4-6
- [ ] References: Numbered, có DOIs
- [ ] Statements & Declarations đầy đủ
- [ ] Figures đúng format và resolution
- [ ] Tables là editable text

## Ethics & Policies

### Authorship (ICMJE Criteria)
1. Substantial contributions to conception/design, data acquisition/analysis
2. Drafted work or revised critically
3. Approved version to be published
4. Accountable for all aspects of the work

### AI/LLM Policy
- LLMs (ChatGPT, etc.) **KHÔNG** đủ tiêu chí authorship
- Sử dụng LLM phải documented trong Methods section
- AI-assisted copy editing (grammar, style) không cần declare
- Phải có human accountability cho final version

### Plagiarism
- Journal sử dụng software để screen plagiarism
- Có thể yêu cầu raw data để verify results
