## Cover Letter Strategy

Trong cover letter, nên nhấn mạnh:

1. **Novelty:** First study kết hợp longitudinal cognition + dual-tracer PET + CSF proteomics để define resilience
2. **Hypothesis-generating:** Emphasize đây là discovery study cần replication
3. **Editorial fit:** Molecular Neurobiology phù hợp vì focus vào molecular mechanisms (proteostasis, innate immunity)
4. **Suggested reviewers:** Nêu rõ lý do chọn mỗi reviewer về expertise match

**Template sentence:**
> "Given the molecular focus of our findings—particularly the enrichment of ubiquitin-proteasome and complement pathways—we respectfully suggest the following reviewers with relevant expertise in CSF proteomics (Dr. Galimberti), neuroinflammation (Dr. Heneka), and synaptic biology (Dr. Huganir)."
