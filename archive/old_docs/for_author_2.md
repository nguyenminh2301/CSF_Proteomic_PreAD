# European Journal of Neuroscience (EJN) - Author Guidelines

**Source:** https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/page/journal/14609568/homepage/forauthors.html

**Journal:** European Journal of Neuroscience (EJN)  
**Publisher:** Wiley (in association with FENS - Federation of European Neuroscience Societies)  
**ISSN:** 1460-9568

---

## 1. Article Types

### Research Report
- **Description:** Reports of new research findings or conceptual analyses that make a significant contribution to knowledge.
- **Abstract:** 250 words, unstructured
- **Requirements:** 
  - Conflict of interest statement
  - Acknowledgments
  - Data availability statement
  - Author contributions
  - Figures and figure legends
  - Graphic abstract and text
  - Ethical statement (if study involves animal or human experiments)

### Short Communication
- **Description:** Should report a significant advance in the field and should represent a 'complete' study rather than preliminary observations.
- **Abstract:** 250 words, unstructured
- **Word Limit:** 3,500 words
- **Figures/Tables:** Max 4 figures and 2 tables
- **Requirements:**
  - Total word count must be indicated on the title page
  - **Strongly discourage the use of supplementary material**
  - Manuscripts not conforming may be returned or re-classified as Research Report
  - COI, Acknowledgments, author contributions, figures and figure legends, graphic abstract, text, and data availability statement required

### Review Article
- **Description:** Full-length articles on topics of particular current interest, including critical reviews, systematic reviews, and meta-analyses.
- **Abstract:** 250 words, unstructured
- **Requirements:** Conflict of interest statement, author contributions statement, graphical abstract and text.

### Special Issue Article / Special Issue Review
- Formatted and considered the same way as regular articles/reviews.
- Same requirements apply.

### Opinion
- **Description:** Brief editorials that discuss issues of topical interest to the neuroscience community.
- **Requirement:** Proposals must be agreed with the Editors-in-Chief before submission.

### Spotlight
- **Description:** Brief reviews that evaluate and discuss novel techniques, controversial research approaches, or issues of validity concerning methodology.
- **Requirement:** Must email editorial office to request submission.

### Commentary
- **Description:** Should integrate the work with other perspectives and offer new insights or raise controversies.
- **Word Limit:** Usually restricted below 1,000 words
- **References:** No more than 6-10 references

### Editorial
- **Description:** Short, invited opinion pieces discussing issues of immediate importance.
- **Note:** EICs will privately contact reviewers/editors for editorials.

### Technical Note
- **Description:** Description of specific techniques, procedures, algorithms, computational methods, or improved protocols.
- **Abstract:** 250 words, unstructured
- **Requirements:** Same as Research Report

### Registered Reports
- **Stage 1:** Includes complete Introduction, Method, and Proposed Analyses
- **Stage 2:** Final manuscript submission after study completion

  - URL: http://mc.manuscriptcentral.com/ejn

### Editorial Contact
- Email: ejneditorial.office@wiley.com

### Submission Fees
- **This journal does not charge submission fees.**

### Peer Review Model
- **Submission:** Single-anonymized model
- **Published papers:** Transparent peer review model
- Reviewer reports, responses, and editor's decision letter will be linked from published article to Web of Science
- Reviewers can choose to remain anonymous or sign their report
- **Opt-out:** Authors can opt-out of transparent peer review during submission

### Appeals and Complaints
- Appeals allowed if rejection based on:
  - Significant misunderstanding of core aspect
  - Failure to understand manuscript's advancement
  - Concerns about manuscript-handling process
- **Not grounds for appeal:** Differences in opinion regarding novelty/significance
- Editor's decision following appeal is final

### Refer & Transfer Program
- If manuscript not accepted, may receive recommendation to transfer to another suitable Wiley journal

## 3. Free Format Submission ("Your Paper Your Way")

EJN uses free format submission for simplified and streamlined process.

### Before You Submit - Requirements:

#### Manuscript File
- Can be single file (including text, figures, tables) OR separate files
- Must contain all required sections: abstract, introduction, methods, results, conclusions
- Figures and tables should have legends
- References may be in any style/format, as long as consistent throughout

**Important:** If manuscript is difficult to read, it will be returned for revision.

#### Title Page Must Include:
1. Brief informative title containing major keywords (no abbreviations)
2. Short running title (< 40 characters)
3. Full names of authors
4. Institutional affiliations where work was conducted
5. Footnote for author's present address (if different)
6. Acknowledgements

#### Required Statements:
1. Data availability statement
2. Funding statement
3. Conflict of interest disclosure
4. Ethics approval statement
5. Patient consent statement
6. Permission to reproduce material from other sources
7. Clinical trial registration
8. Co-author details (affiliation and email)
9. ORCID ID (required - https://orcid.org)

### At Revision Stage
Manuscripts MUST be submitted in accordance with author guidelines at revision stage. Must include:
- Two copies of manuscript:
  1. One clean copy
  2. One marked changes copy (without tracked changes)

## 5. Journal Format Requirements

### Title Page
- Brief informative title with major keywords (no abbreviations)
- Short running title (< 40 characters)
- Full names of authors
- Institutional affiliations
- Present address footnote (if different)
- Acknowledgements

### Abstract
- **Maximum 250 words**
- **Unstructured** (no headings)

### Figures
- Supply as separate files
- Review basic figure requirements: https://authorservices.wiley.com/asset/photos/electronic_artwork_guidelines.pdf
- Post-acceptance figure requirements apply

### Statistical Methods - IMPORTANT

#### Requirements:
- **Complete description of statistical methods is REQUIRED**
- Follow EJN Editorial on Reporting Statistical Methods and Results (EJN 2008, 28, 2363-2364)
- Checklist available: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/pb-assets/assets/14609568/Statistics_checklist-1509476297000.pdf

#### Results and Statistical Analyses:
- Present observations with minimal reference to earlier literature or interpretations
- Main statistical results reported in Results section
- Must include:
  - Proper statistical term (e.g., F statistic)
  - Degrees of freedom
  - Precise p-value
- Figure captions: limit statistical descriptions to important post hoc comparisons

#### **Graphical Guidelines - IMPORTANT:**
- **EJN discourages bar charts**
- **Recommended:** Boxplots or scatterplots instead
- Editorial reference: "Graphical Representation of Statistical Analyses" (https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/ejn.13400/full)

## 6. Research Ethics Guidelines

### Human Studies
- Include IRB approvals
- Ethical treatment of research participants
- Informed consent documentation
- Explicit statement identifying approving Institution/Review Committee
- Permit number preferred

### Animal Studies
- Specify: species, strain, sex, age, supplier, numbers of animals
- Species should be identified in Title or Abstract
- Follow Council Directive 2010/63EU or NIH Guidelines
- For genetically modified mice: follow Crusio et al., Genes, Brain and Behavior (2009) 8:1–4

### Ethics Statement Requirements
- Editors reserve right to reject if appropriate procedures not followed
- Journal follows COPE (Committee on Publication Ethics) core practices
- Uses iThenticate's CrossCheck for plagiarism detection
